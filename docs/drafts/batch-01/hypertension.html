2025 High Blood Pressure Guideline - Top Things to Know

The 2025 US guideline sets an overarching treatment goal below 130/80 mm Hg, with individual considerations for institutional care, limited life expectancy and pregnancy.

Official AHA Top Things to Know, item 1; updated August 14, 2025.

Outpatient management of essential hypertension: a review based on the latest clinical guidelines (2024)

Blood-pressure targets differ across guidelines; distinguish the guideline, risk population and treatment tolerance when applying a target.

Treatment goals section; Table 2 summarizes older guideline editions.

X1: SPRINT (2015)

Candidate X1, not yet a verified guideline-citation link. SPRINT tested systolic targets below 120 versus below 140; it did not directly test below 130/80 for every adult.

P
9,361 adults aged at least 50, systolic BP 130-180 mm Hg and increased cardiovascular risk; diabetes and prior stroke excluded; 102 US/Puerto Rico sites.
I
Treatment targeting systolic BP below 120 mm Hg.
C
Treatment targeting systolic BP below 140 mm Hg.
O
MI, other ACS, stroke, heart failure or cardiovascular death: 1.65% versus 2.19% per year; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.64-0.89). Mortality HR 0.73 (0.60-0.90). More serious hypotension, syncope, electrolyte abnormalities and AKI/failure with intensive treatment; no increase in injurious falls reported.
T
Median follow-up 3.26 years; intervention stopped early for benefit. These are 2015 primary-report results, not the 2021 re-adjudicated estimates.

Open-label treatment with blinded outcome adjudication, early stopping and selected population limit generalization. Structured BP measurement differs from casual office readings. Do not extrapolate directly to diabetes, prior stroke, younger adults or nursing-home residents.
